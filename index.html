INTRODUCING PRINTHIVE

A more connected way to print.

PrintHive is building a technology platform designed to bring people who need printing and local print shops closer together. Our vision is to make printing more convenient through a simpler, more connected experience.

Printing should feel easier.

Finding the right print shop

Finding a suitable local print shop and understanding the available options can take unnecessary effort.

Managing print requirements

Preparing documents and communicating printing requirements can become a fragmented experience.

Connecting digital to physical

Getting a digital file printed should feel like a simpler, more connected journey.

These are the problems PrintHive aims to address, not findings from completed research.

OUR VISION

One connected printing ecosystem.

We envision a simpler connection between people who need printing and the local businesses that make it happen.

Simpler printing

Make the process of submitting and managing printing requirements more convenient.

Local connections

Help customers discover and connect with suitable participating print shops nearby.

Smarter workflows

Explore technology-driven workflows that can make printing easier for customers and print-shop owners.

These are planned goals. None of these features are available yet.
